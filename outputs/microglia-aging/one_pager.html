What the run found

  • 50 of 351 in-scope studies (14.2%) include both young and old baseline samples with at least 3 per group.
  • Age can be read for 26.2% of 27004 expression samples; it was written as 555 different strings under 13 different field names.
  • 26.4% of expression samples do not state sex.
  • 8 in-scope studies use cell lines rather than primary tissue or cells, which a keyword search alone does not reveal.

Method

NCBI GEO search, then rule-based harmonization of free-text sample metadata into age, sex, tissue, cell type and strain, keeping the raw text and rule behind every value.

Score = weighted completeness, design fit, data type and traceability. Weights live in a YAML file.

8 of 10 automated checks passed.

Reproducibility

Every NCBI response is stored, so the run replays offline with identical results.
